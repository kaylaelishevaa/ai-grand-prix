Nook mobile call prototype

Open / or /camera. Both use the phone-first incoming-call interface. Answer video call starts the camera, followed by the real AI audio connection. With "Let Nori see my camera" enabled, compressed snapshots go directly to OpenAI through the Realtime data channel about every three seconds and on spoken/typed requests. An active try-on sends a separate labeled preview alongside the real scene. This is image understanding, not a continuous video stream. The onscreen AI presence is an animated orb, not a remote human video stream.

The camera-sharing pill shows server receipt and can pause/resume sharing without stopping local try-on. No images are sent before the call connects, with sharing off, with the camera off, or from a hidden page. The application does not write audio or frames to storage. It keeps at most two acknowledged snapshot messages in model context; removing context is not a provider data-retention guarantee. The look_at_camera tool asks for a fresh view when needed. Nori uses visual context for catalog search and supported approximate overlay adjustments.

Products: 18 sourced Coupang listings, three each for lipstick, chairs, desk lamps, T-shirts, hoodies and glasses, with original listing images stored locally and historical reference prices. The “18 picks” sheet lets a shopper test each group without starting a voice call. At most two transcript bubbles remain on screen. Touch swipe left skips; right saves to an in-memory demo bag (no order/payment). Product Details can be explained by Nori or opened on Coupang. Try it loads the selected product photo. T-shirts and hoodies use body-aligned product-image warping. Glasses track the eye line and nose with roll/yaw adjustment and transparent lens interiors. Chairs and lamps remain approximate manually positioned product previews. Near-white connected backgrounds are removed locally while interior product pixels are preserved. Lipstick colour is estimated from the selected listing image, not calibrated colour or finish. Voice controls supported size, opacity, position and rotation; colour changes are limited to lipstick estimates. No adjustment panel. The deck retains personalized recommendations when overlays change.

Hand swipes: local MediaPipe HandLandmarker 0.10.14, open-palm motion, mirrored correctly. Starts after products appear with camera active; toggle via the hand icon. At most10fps, cooldown and neutral reset to reduce accidental actions. Touch/button alternatives always available. Camera, microphone, and tracking resources stop when ending or leaving the call.

Files: site-pages/camera.html, public/mobile-call.css, mobile-call.js, mobile-call-state.js, hand-gestures.js, camera-snapshots.js, product-preview.js. Reuses camera-engine.js and voice-call.js. Backend lib/nook-voice.mjs supports look_at_camera, product_action and set_tryon controls.

Validation: npm run check; Sites build helper. Camera/audio permissions and hand gesture accuracy depend on the phone/browser and require physical device testing. Catalog availability and actual product fit are not verified.

Try-on automatically minimizes the product card, header details and secondary controls. At most two small transcript bubbles remain; Products or More brings back the card and controls without stopping the preview. Flip stays visible with microphone and hang-up. It requests the opposite camera using strict facing-mode constraints, and restores the original camera with an explanation if no other camera is available. Microphone/voice session is not restarted by Flip. Front-camera mirroring is automatic; there is no mirror control.

The generated-still-photo feature and Images API route are removed. There is no upload or image-generation action. The existing shopper-controlled camera snapshots continue to help Nori understand the live scene. All try-on rendering happens locally and remains approximate; it is not cloth physics, measured sizing or full 3D room reconstruction.

public/product-catalog.csv lists all 18 exact product names, IDs, types, source URLs, reference prices and image paths.
